                KNEC ENTREPRENEURSHIP BUSINESS PLAN TEMPLATE  
           Craft, Diploma and Higher Diploma.  
                                              How to use this template  
           Delete this page and all grey Guidance lines before printing.  
           Replace every [bracketed placeholder] with your own work. Write all text yourself.  
           The layout follows the KNEC Entrepreneurship Project Report Outline.  
           Use your own survey, your own price quotes and your own numbers. A plan built on real evidence is easy to defend.  
           Use the companion Excel financial model for Chapter Five.  
       
              Format required by KNEC  
                A4 paper, Times New Roman, font size 12, 1.5 line spacing. This template is already set up that way.  
                Bound and labelled. Two copies: one for the institution, one for you.  
                Signed declarations by you and your supervisor.  
                APA referencing.  
              Marks  
                Business idea: title and rationale 5, defence and approval 10.  
                Plan: Synopsis 5, Chapter One 10, Two 10, Three 15, Four 15, Five 15.  
                Business proposal: defence 10, final report submission 5.  
              Do not  
                Do not copy another business plan.  
                Do not invent survey results or prices.  
                Do not make figures in the text differ from the financial tables.
